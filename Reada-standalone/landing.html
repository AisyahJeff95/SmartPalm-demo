About PalmNex

Every hectare is different. Fertiliser decisions should be too.

Estate-wide averages can hide real differences in crop nutrient condition. PalmNex gives agronomists and estate teams a map of that variation, so fertiliser planning can focus where it matters, backed by field validation. PalmNex does not ask you to read satellite imagery: it interprets the AI output and turns it into nutrient status and fertiliser recommendations.
