User Manual — SmartPalm Command Center

1. Command Center Map (All 20 Comprehensive Boundaries)

Click any estate pin or boundary on the dark map (or select from the Left Panel / Top Dropdown) to zoom to its blocks and inspect its Stress Rating and 3-Month N, P, K, Mg, Ca, B trend.

Click + Schedule Fertilizer in the Right Panel to log your fertilizer application date. SmartPalm automatically schedules the follow-up satellite prediction (+30, +45, or +60 days post-application) and notifies you when the next prediction is due.
