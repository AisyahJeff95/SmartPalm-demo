About PalmNex

Every hectare is different. Fertiliser decisions should be too.

Estate-wide averages can hide real differences in crop nutrient condition. PalmNex gives agronomists and estate teams a 10m pixel map of that variation, so fertiliser planning can focus where it matters, backed by field validation.

Click any 10m pixel inside the irregular estate boundary to inspect its live foliar N, P, K, Mg, B, and Ca levels and canopy stress meter.
